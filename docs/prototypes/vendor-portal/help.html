Common questions

Press a question to see the answer.

💰 When do I get paid?▸
Payouts go out twice a month, on the 1st and the 15th. You can see exactly how much is waiting for you on the My Money page. If you ever need it sooner, just call us and ask for an early payout.
✂ How much does the mall keep?▸
The mall keeps a small commission on each sale. Your rate is written in your agreement. You never have to do the math yourself — every sale on the What Sold page shows the shopper’s price, the commission, and exactly what you keep.
🔄 What if a shopper returns something?▸
Returns are completely normal. When something comes back, it shows up on your What Sold page as a red number with a minus sign. The commission is returned to you too, so you are never out of pocket.
🏷 How do I add more items to my booth?▸
Bring your items to the mall office and we will check them in for you. Each item gets a little tag so the register knows it is yours. You can bring as many as you like.
🗺 Can I move to a bigger booth?▸
Yes! Take a look at the My Booth page. Any booth shown in white is open right now. Call us and we will help you move.
🔒 Are these numbers correct?▸
Yes, always. Every number you see comes straight from the mall’s official accounting books. They are checked every day, so what you see is exactly what you are owed. There is no guesswork anywhere.
📋 The text is too small for me.▸
No problem at all. Press the Bigger Text button at the very top of any page and everything gets larger. Your choice is remembered, so it stays big on every page you visit.
🔊 Can someone read the page to me?▸
Yes. Press the Read This Page button at the top and your device will read the whole page out loud. Press it again to stop.
